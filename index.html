WordKart Privacy Policy

App: WordKart (Android, package com.wordkart.app) · Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short
  • WordKart has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your learning progress stays on your phone.
  • The microphone is used only while you tap Record in the speaking practice. Recordings stay on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • There are no ads and nothing to buy in the app.
  • WordKart is not meant for children under 13.

1. Who we are

WordKart is a vocabulary learning app for German and French, published by Pankaj Khobragade ("we", "us"). You can contact us at pankaj.drei@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The app works offline and needs no sign-up. The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage:

  • your learning progress (words learned, review dates, finished days, streaks, badges, exam results, grammar progress);
  • your settings (for example theme, daily goal, speech speed, reminder time);
  • small technical markers, such as the day an ad was last shown and whether ads have been removed.

We do not receive this data and we do not have a server that stores it. It is deleted when you use Reset progress in Settings (settings are kept) or when you uninstall the app.

Backups. In Settings you can create a backup of your progress as text and share it with an app of your choice. You decide where it goes; we never see it. Your phone's Android backup may also copy the app's data to your Google account, depending on your device settings. That is handled by Google under your own Google account, not by us.

3. Microphone and speaking practice

The speaking practice and the milestone speaking parts let you record yourself reading a sentence and play it back. The app asks for the microphone permission the first time you tap Record, and uses it only while a recording is running. Recordings are kept on your phone for playback and are not uploaded, shared or analysed by us. You can switch the permission off at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

4. Speech (text-to-speech)

The app reads words and sentences aloud using your phone's own text-to-speech service. Depending on your phone and the voice you have installed, that service (for example Google's) may process the text on the device or online, under its own privacy policy. The app does not record or store this audio.

5. Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone at the time you chose. It is created and shown locally; no push notification server is involved.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not show ads, sell anything in the app, or use analytics or tracking services.
  • The app does not use your phone's advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

8. Children

WordKart is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us.

9. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is usually nothing for us to look up or delete: your progress is deleted with Reset progress or by uninstalling the app. You can turn the microphone and notification permissions off at any time in your phone's settings.

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection rights (such as access, correction, deletion, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority), you can write to us at pankaj.drei@gmail.com with any question about your data.

10. Security

Your progress is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. The app does not send your progress anywhere. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the amount of data to a minimum.

11. Changes

If the app's data practices change (for example if ads or an in-app purchase are added later), we will update this page before the new version is released and the date above, and the updated policy applies from then on.
