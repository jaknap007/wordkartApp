WordKart Privacy Policy

App: WordKart (Android, package com.wordkart.app) · Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short
  • WordKart has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your learning progress stays on your phone.
  • The microphone is used only while you tap Record in the speaking practice. Recordings stay on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • After your first days in each level the app may show a short ad from Google AdMob. You can remove ads for good by buying the optional Supporter purchase.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • WordKart is not meant for children under 13.

1. Who we are

WordKart is a vocabulary learning app for German and French, published by pankaj Khobragade ("we", "us"). You can contact us at pankaj.drei@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The app works offline and needs no sign-up. The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage:

  • your learning progress (words learned, review dates, finished days, streaks, badges, exam results, grammar progress);
  • your settings (for example theme, daily goal, speech speed, reminder time);
  • small technical markers, such as the day an ad was last shown and whether ads have been removed.

We do not receive this data and we do not have a server that stores it. It is deleted when you use Reset progress in Settings (settings are kept) or when you uninstall the app.

Backups. In Settings you can create a backup of your progress as text and share it with an app of your choice. You decide where it goes; we never see it. Your phone's Android backup may also copy the app's data to your Google account, depending on your device settings. That is handled by Google under your own Google account, not by us.

3. Microphone and speaking practice

The speaking practice and the milestone speaking parts let you record yourself reading a sentence and play it back. The app asks for the microphone permission the first time you tap Record, and uses it only while a recording is running. Recordings are kept on your phone for playback and are not uploaded, shared or analysed by us. You can switch the permission off at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

4. Speech (text-to-speech)

The app reads words and sentences aloud using your phone's own text-to-speech service. Depending on your phone and the voice you have installed, that service (for example Google's) may process the text on the device or online, under its own privacy policy. The app does not record or store this audio.

5. Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone at the time you chose. It is created and shown locally; no push notification server is involved.

6. Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep WordKart free, the app may show a short full-screen ad. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can reveal an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app first shows Google's consent form, and ads are only requested if you agree. You can change your advertising choices in your phone's settings (for example by resetting or deleting your advertising ID).

The app is not directed at children, and we tell the ad service that the user is not a child. More about how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

The optional "Supporter" purchase ("Gift us a coffee") removes ads for good. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To unlock the purchase and let you restore it on a new phone, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, the product you bought, purchase and receipt information, and basic technical data such as your app version, device type and IP address. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Your purchase also stays on your Google account, which is how "Restore purchases" works after reinstalling.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not run accounts, analytics or advertising networks of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

10. Children

WordKart is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us.

11. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is usually nothing for us to look up or delete: your progress is deleted with Reset progress or by uninstalling the app. You can withdraw ad consent in your phone's settings, or turn the microphone and notification permissions off at any time.

The ad service and the purchase service handle the data described above under their own policies. If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection rights (such as access, correction, deletion, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority), you can exercise them with those services or write to us at pankaj.drei@gmail.com and we will help as far as we can, including asking RevenueCat to delete the anonymous purchase record linked to your app user ID.

12. Security

Your progress is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Data sent to the ad and purchase services travels over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the amount of data to a minimum.

13. Changes

If the app's data practices change (for example a new feature or a new service), we will update this page and the date above, and the updated policy applies from then on.
